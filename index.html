What We Do

On-Site Audit

We spend 2-3 days at your property as a guest, assessing your watersports operation from the guest perspective. You receive a detailed report covering equipment, instruction quality, safety briefings, and the overall experience arc.

Fleet Advisory

If your kit is holding your guests back, we'll tell you exactly what to change and why. We supply performance SUP, wing foil, and foil boards directly to resort properties through our equipment division.

Experience Design

From how guests are briefed to how sessions are structured, small changes in delivery make a measurable difference to satisfaction scores and repeat bookings.
